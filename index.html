Our Finance

A private money tracker for the two of you. Everything is encrypted on this phone before it's saved, so GitHub only ever stores scrambled data.

Overview

Balances

Everyday money in each account right now (savings are on the Savings tab). Tap an account to correct it if it doesn't match your bank app.

Household spending and income. Tap a spending category to give it a monthly budget.

Loans

Money you lent to or borrowed from people. Lending and paying back are transfers, so they never count as spending or income.

Add a person

Someone you lend money to or borrow from, e.g. a friend or relative.

Each business has its own money. Investing in a business is a transfer from your joint or personal account, so it isn't counted as household spending. Profit = business income − business expenses (like staff salary).

Add a business

Creates a separate account for it. Then use Invest to move money in.

Money you set aside, like your cash emergency fund. It's kept apart from your everyday balances, and spending from it doesn't count in the monthly budget. Putting money in or taking it out is a transfer.

Add a savings pot

e.g. Cash savings (THB), Emergency fund (MMK), Trip fund. One per currency or goal.

Category
Who is it for?

Rent, phone plans, subscriptions. Add one with + and choose how often it repeats. When it's due, it shows up on Overview. Tap a bill to change its amount, e.g. when the rent goes up.

The two of you

Names used for "who is it for" and for settling up.

Take each person's share automatically from
When one of you pays from your own account, the other person's part is taken straight from the account picked here, so nothing piles up in Settle up. Choose "Don't take it" to track it as a debt instead.

Accounts

Owner: Joint means shared money, so spending from it never creates a debt. Pick a person for an account that's only theirs, and the app tracks who owes whom.
Type: Bank / wallet is everyday money. Savings lives in the Savings tab, outside your balances and budget. Business keeps money out of household totals and shows profit. Person is for loans.
Target: optional savings goal, shown as a progress bar.
Closed: for an account you no longer use. It disappears from lists but its history stays.

Export to a spreadsheet

Download entries as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. The file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

Picking a single account adds a running balance column, handy for checking against your bank statement.

Full backup

A complete copy of everything in one file (not encrypted). Keep one somewhere safe now and then. You can restore it into a new repo or a future version of this app.

Change passphrase

Re-encrypts all your data with a new passphrase. The other phone will ask for the new one next time it opens. Old versions in GitHub's history stay locked with the old passphrase.

GitHub connection
